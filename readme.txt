This is Hectors gift this year. I am building him a custom slot machine designed to
be a pc plugin so it sits on his desktop when we play games together.

The components will be the following

- Raspberry pi pico
- Servo to push the cards out of the slot in the front
- Display to show the 'slots' that are going to either line up or fail
- button to play the slots with

The goal is to start this with the following

1 in 5 for the first small gift card.

1 in 50 for the next 3

1 in 200 for the next 2

1 in 500 for the next 2

1 in 1500 for the last 2

Small speaker for sound effects

This is supposed to last him a while, but be much more fair than an actual slot machine

If it all goes right, it should be compact enough to stay on your desk at work or while
gaming without being too much of a nuisance and be kind of a fun little
distraction from time to time. I am hoping this goes well and it can be a great part of my
Christmas portfolio. I could even see making this for other people some day.
